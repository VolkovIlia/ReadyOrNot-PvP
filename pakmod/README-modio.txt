Ready or Not PvP - SWAT vs Suspects (ALPHA, in-game Mods menu version)
======================================================================

This is an alpha: expect bugs. Please report them at
https://github.com/VolkovIlia/ReadyOrNot-PvP/issues

INSTALL
- Host: subscribe to the mod in the game's Mods menu (or put RoNPvP.pak into
  <game folder>\ReadyOrNot\Content\Paks\~mods\) and restart the game.
- Friends: either subscribe to the mod too, or turn on
  Settings > Game > "Join modded servers" once; otherwise the host's lobby is not visible.

HOW TO PLAY
The host starts any co-op mission: the "Choose your team" menu opens on the pre-mission
screen, in the game's language (the language button switches between English and Russian).
Other players pick a side in chat: !swat or !suspect. A click on a bot slot of the SWAT card
takes a bot away or adds one (up to five).
The host's menu also picks the rules: vanilla (guns and armour as the mission's AI have them)
or balanced (rifles for suspects, Kevlar for the AI SWAT squad). Suspects open any door and
pick up dropped guns (hold the evidence-collect key on a gun).
In a round hold Tab for the scoreboard; the kill feed is in chat.

The mode's log for bug reports: %LOCALAPPDATA%\ReadyOrNot\Saved\SaveGames\RoNPvPLog.sav
Details: https://github.com/VolkovIlia/ReadyOrNot-PvP
